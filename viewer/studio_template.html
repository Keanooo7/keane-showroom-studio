    Specification

    Sizes and quantities are taken from the showroom display's CAD model. Pictures are live renders of this build.

    How this model is built

    • Geometry: fitted from five showroom photos. Doors and drawers are rebuilt as Shaker fronts (2-1/4 in frame, 1/4 in recessed panel, 1.6 mm edge radius), slim Shaker (1 in frame) or slab fronts. Drawer fronts follow the door style, or the short ones can be flat slabs. Countertops are re-extruded from the CAD footprint with a 1.5 mm eased edge.
    • Finish library: 38 Decore DRS131 finishes (decore.com), 34 Vadara quartz designs (vadara.uk) and 48 MSI granites (msisurfaces.com). Each texture loads only when picked.
    • Hardware: the showroom bar pull (matched to the photos) or any Top Knobs knob or pull, built from the maker's spec table and product photos at true size. Doors and drawers can take different pieces. Each piece loads only when picked.
    • Scale: Vadara slabs map at 126 × 63 in. MSI close-ups are assumed to show 24 × 24 in, and Decore chips about 10 in.
    • Live view: PBR materials, a studio HDR light room, soft shadows, ambient occlusion and Khronos PBR Neutral tone mapping for true product colour.
    • Photo render: path tracing with an edge-preserving denoise. It converges in about a minute and stops at 400 samples.
