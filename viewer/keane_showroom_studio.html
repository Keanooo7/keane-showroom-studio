How this model is built

  • Geometry: fitted from five showroom photos. Doors and drawers are rebuilt as Shaker fronts (2-1/4 in frame, 1/4 in recessed panel, 1.6 mm edge radius). Countertops are re-extruded from the CAD footprint with a 1.5 mm eased edge.
  • Finish library: 38 Decore DRS131 finishes (decore.com), 34 Vadara quartz designs (vadara.uk) and 48 MSI granites (msisurfaces.com). Each texture loads only when picked.
  • Scale: Vadara slabs map at 126 × 63 in. MSI close-ups are assumed to show 24 × 24 in, and Decore chips about 10 in.
  • Live view: PBR materials, a studio HDR light room, soft shadows, ambient occlusion and Khronos PBR Neutral tone mapping for true product colour.
  • Photo render: path tracing with an edge-preserving denoise. It converges in about a minute and stops at 400 samples.
