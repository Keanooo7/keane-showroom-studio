# Kitchen Capture Protocol (iPhone), one page

**Goal:** one slow 3–5 minute video that sees every cabinet surface from 3 heights. Bad capture can't be fixed later in software. Takes about 15 minutes, start to finish.

## Before you film (5 min)
- **Clear the counters.** Move appliances, dish racks, mail, magnets and towels out of the room or into one corner that you won't film. Close all doors and drawers.
- **Lights:** turn on every light, including under-cabinet lights. Close blinds if sun falls on the counters, because bright patches and hard shadows ruin the model. Don't film at dusk.
- **Scale reference (required):**
  - Tape the printed **ArUco/AprilTag board** (letter size, from the office kit) flat on the countertop near a corner. Don't fold it or cover it.
  - Write down the **countertop height** (floor to top of counter, usually 36 in) and **one long run** (wall to the end of the run).
  - Put the job name and both numbers in the upload note.
- **Matte helpers (optional):** for glossy white or quartz, stick 6–10 small pieces of **blue painter's tape** in random spots on doors and counters. They give the software texture to lock onto.

## Phone settings
- Use the **main 1× lens**. Don't use 0.5× ultra-wide, and don't zoom.
- Video: **4K at 30 fps**. Turn **off** Cinematic, Action mode, HDR video and Night mode.
- **Lock exposure and focus:** point at a mid-grey cabinet and press and hold until **"AE/AF LOCK"** appears. Don't tap again while filming.
- Clean the lens. Hold the phone **upright (portrait)** for the whole video, and keep it that way.

## Filming: 3 passes, slow (about a half step per second)
1. **High pass:** phone held overhead, tilted about 30° down. Walk the full length of the cabinets, about 1.5–2 m away. This pass sees the countertop surface, the tops of the uppers and the crown.
2. **Middle pass:** phone at chest height, pointed straight at the cabinets. Walk the full length back.
3. **Low pass:** crouch with the phone at knee height, tilted about 20° up. Walk the full length again. This pass sees the **toe kicks, the undersides of the uppers, the undersides of drawers and the front edge of the counter**.
- **Corners and ends:** at every inside corner, end panel, fridge gap and appliance opening, stop and slowly **arc 90° around it**, so the camera sees each side.
- **Overlap:** each step should keep about **2/3 of the previous view** in frame. Never sweep the camera fast. If the picture blurs, go back and redo that part.
- **Closer detail pass (30 s):** at about 0.5 m, slowly film the handles, hinges, door seams and the counter edge profile from 2 angles each.
- **Faucet, stainless and mirrors:** film them briefly and from an angle. Don't stand straight in front of a reflection.

## Don'ts
- Don't let people or pets walk through the shot, and stay out of mirrors and reflections.
- Don't open or close doors while filming. Don't change the lights mid-video.
- Don't stand in one spot and pan around (rotation only). **Walk**, so each view is seen from a slightly different position.

## Check before you leave (2 min)
- Play the video back. It should be sharp, evenly bright, show every cabinet face, and the scale board should be visible in at least 3 seconds of it.
- Video length: about **3–5 min**. The software pulls about 200 frames from it, so longer is fine, but shaky is not.
- Upload the video plus the note: `job name, countertop height, long-run length, scale board yes/no`.
